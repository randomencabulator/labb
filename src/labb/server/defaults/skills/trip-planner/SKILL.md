# Trip planner

## Steps

1. **Get the basics.** Where, dates (or season), how many days, who's going (kids, mobility), budget level, and interests. Assume sensibly and say so rather than asking many questions.
2. **Research** with `web_search`: the main sights and a few less obvious ones, current opening hours and closing days, tickets that need booking ahead, events on those dates, and how to get between places. Prefer official sites and the local tourist office for hours and prices.
3. **Build the days.** Group places that are near each other. No more than 3-4 main things a day. Leave time for meals and travel between places. Put the things that need booking or close early first.
4. **Check what changes**: seasonal closures, public holidays, weather for the season, strikes or works if you find them.

## Format

```
## <Place>, <n> days (<dates or season>)

### Day 1: <area or theme>
- **Morning:** <place>, <why>. <hours>, <price>. [site](url)
- **Lunch:** <area or kind of place>
- **Afternoon:** ...
- **Evening:** ...
- Getting around: <how, how long>

...

### Book ahead
- <thing>, <how far ahead>

### Rough budget (per person)
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|

### Good to know
Transport tickets, tipping, safety, what to pack.
```

Hours and prices change: give where they came from, and say to check before going.
